Square Keeper

Role: Gameplay Programmer (main programmer, 3-person university team)

Technologies: Unity, C#

Status: Released June 2022, published by Jandusoft

A puzzle game based on a card system, where each card lets the player execute different actions — like a sword swing — as the hero tries to escape a dungeon filled with enemies and traps. Commercially released across multiple platforms.

Awards: Winner, Indie Burger Developer Awards 2022. Best Second Year's Game at ENTI's Demoday 2021.

Catography

Role: Gameplay Programmer

Technologies: Unity, C#

A collectathon platformer with a cute, cozy aesthetic — and most importantly, filled with kittens! Your goal is helping the cats who live on Arcadia and creating a map for the island as its cartographer cat.

Awards: Honorable Mention, Bachelor's Final Project at ENTI's Demoday.
